Fluid Expressions

If you had a chance to make art in space,
what would you bring?
what would you make?

Journal 01 | T-132 Days

Life on earth provides us with experience and intuition about how fluids will behave, and what they can be used for. Similarly, we have some intuition for what constitutes liquid-based art, as well as brushes, paints, and canvases. These intuitions can be productively challenged when considering liquid-based art in space. How will microgravity affect the experience of applying paint to canvas? Can the experience of creating art with liquid-based media be re-envisioned to utilize and reflect the unique properties of space?

Journal 02 | T-85 Days

The goal of this project is to develop a novel set of instruments for manipulating liquid-based media in space. To do this, I am using a series of questions to challenge current intuition —

+ How might we reconsider the brush for microgravity environments?
+ How might we reconsider the canvas in 3D space?
+ What can be used as pigment or ink? 
+ How might we capture the essence of space and microgravity?
+ What can be portrayed through art made in space?
