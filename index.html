Fluid Expressions

Life on Earth provides us with experience and intuition about what constitutes artwork made with water — what a brush is, what paints are, what can serve as a canvas.

When imagining making art off-planet, can we re-envision how we make art to utilize and reflect the unique properties of space? How do we stay tethered to Earth and home from so far away?

Journal 01 | T-262 Days

I have an opportunity to do something I never considered possible. I won’t be the first person to make art in space — art, music, and craft have all found their way to low Earth orbit since the beginning of our presence off-planet — but my job, my purpose for being up there will be to make art. A commander, pilot, mission specialists, and me… an unusual crew we'll make.

The most emphatic piece of advice I’ve received is to be present, to experience the sensation of weightlessness and commit it to memory. But how can I convey what I feel in that moment through what I create? How do I capture that memory so that I can share it with others?

Journal 02 | T-230 Days

The goal of this project is to develop a novel set of instruments for manipulating liquid-based media in space. To do this, I am using a series of questions to challenge current intuition —

+ How might we reconsider the brush for microgravity environments?
+ How might we reconsider the canvas in 3D space?
+ What can be used as pigment or ink? 
+ How might we capture the essence of space and microgravity?
+ What can be portrayed through art made in space?
